## TL;DR for ranking
Fanfic-first reader. Rank hardest on **franchise match**, then **trope match**,
then use rating/voters as a soft up-weight. **Never hard-cut on rating** — low
rated books can be gems, just rank them lower. No per-franchise cap.

## Top franchises
List the franchises you read most, roughly in priority order, e.g.:
1. Marvel
2. Naruto
3. (your favorites here)

## Loved tropes
system / leveling, rebirth & reincarnation, transmigration, villain / anti-hero,
cheat / overpowered MC, apocalypse / survival. (Edit to taste.)

## Power & progression
Describe pacing you like — e.g. "min-maxer, dislikes slow gradual low-level
grinds; prefers fast/early power spikes and smart use of foreknowledge."

## Original (non-fanfic) novels — high bar
Only surface originals that are very clickbaity. List the hooks that work for you
(system, apocalypse, villain, reincarnation, sci-fi, cultivation, …).

(already owned), not as a positive/negative taste signal.
